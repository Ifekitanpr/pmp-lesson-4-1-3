# Lesson 4.1.3 illustration prompts

The ten concrete story-set assets below were generated with the built-in ChatGPT image-generation tool and saved in `src/assets/illustrations/`. The original four abstract concepts are retained at the end for provenance but are not used in the lesson.

## Concrete story set used in the lesson

Every prompt below includes this shared direction:

> Style: flat story-set editorial illustration with recognizable human characters and a concrete project-management situation, simple shapes, professional and meaningful, not an infographic and not abstract. Plain pure white background, no border, no container, no shadow, no gradient. Brand palette only: bright blue #007BFF, deep navy #0F172A, orange #FF6B35, cool grey #94A3B8, white. Landscape 3:2 composition, generous whitespace, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark.

### `hook-story-v2.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: Lesson 4.1.3 hero. A project manager sits at a desk overwhelmed by tall stacks of unread project reports, dashboards and status pages spilling around them. The manager looks past the clutter toward a small, clearly organized set of five essential decision cards highlighted in blue and orange on the right. The visual story must immediately communicate: lots of reporting can hide loss of control, while only a few signals truly matter.

### `filter-story-v2.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: filtering deliberately lesson scene. A project manager stands before a large board crowded with many grey project data cards and deliberately selects only a few essential cards, placing those chosen cards into a concise blue decision brief for a sponsor. Show an intentional human filtering action, not a literal funnel.

### `kpi-story.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: KPI category card illustration. A project manager and team member review a simple performance board showing milestone progress, schedule movement, quality status and target tracking; the project manager points to a gauge moving outside its target range and prepares a decision. Concrete workplace scene, no readable labels.

### `risk-threshold-story.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: risk-threshold category card illustration. A project manager and sponsor face a clearly visible orange tripwire boundary on a risk board; one risk marker has crossed the boundary and the project manager immediately escalates it to the sponsor. Make the predefined trigger and resulting action unmistakable.

### `budget-constraint-story.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: budget-constraint category card illustration. A project manager and financial analyst review a budget board with coin stacks and a spending line approaching a firm orange limit; they actively discuss re-scoping before the limit is exceeded. Concrete project decision scene.

### `progress-report-story.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: progress-report category card illustration. A project manager presents a concise structured project status update to a mixed group of stakeholders around a table, highlights one emerging issue on a simple visual board, and shows the adjustment being made; the stakeholders are visibly aligned and ready to act.

### `compliance-story-v2.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: compliance information lesson illustration. A project team near the end of a project discovers an overlooked regulatory and quality checklist; a compliance specialist points to a missed requirement while the project manager and team immediately plan corrective action. The missed compliance obligation and the urgency of remediation must be unmistakable, concrete, and human-centered.

### `predictive-story.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: predictive delivery illustration. A project manager leads a planned phase-gate review with a small team gathered around a fixed milestone board and calendar; they review the scheduled project report at an intentional control point before approving the next phase. Concrete workplace scene showing planned reporting cadence, not an abstract timeline.

### `adaptive-story.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: adaptive delivery illustration. A Scrum team holds a daily stand-up beside a task board with simple cards and a burndown-style trend; the product owner and team reassess which information matters for today's decisions and move one priority card. Concrete collaborative agile workplace scene, not an abstract process diagram.

### `precision-story-v2.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: lesson synthesis illustration. A project manager sits at a decision table receiving five concise, recognizable project inputs from team members: performance, risk, budget, progress, and compliance. The project manager compares the five inputs and makes one confident decision with the sponsor. Show clarity and control through a concrete human workplace scene, not a funnel and not an abstract infographic.
